## Scope conclusion

The corrected roadmap Phase 2 and `AC-V2-ADMIN-01A` cover complete Full Course,
Quant, Verbal and DI authoring, separate optional live events, whole-course
publication, independent batches, private Meet details and enrolled-only schedule
visibility. They do not require completing the entire Student content journey.

work. Independent/universal material publication, recording replacement/deletion
and a complete new-tab Student preview are Phase 5 work. Real source migration
remains a separately reviewed operation. Keeping these open does not mean the
local Phase 2 Admin workflow must be rebuilt.

## Findings to reconcile in the delivery handoff

1. **Current versus historical status:** the starting README and release guide
   intermixed old candidate heads, five-migration lists and a claim that Student
   eligibility was unresolved. The current implementation has six proposed Phase 2
   migrations and an enrolled-only reader. The delivery handoff must identify one
   exact candidate, three feature flags and current scope, preserving dated
   historical records as history. Current preflight source already lists 12
   tables and 17 routines; the older guide's counts are obsolete.
2. **Eight seats is metadata, not enforced allocation:** `liveRegistrationLimit`
   publishes eight for live courses. `set_v2_batch_enrollment` manages schedule
   visibility, not paid live registration, and does not enforce a seat cap.
   Allocation belongs to Phase 3. Do not report registration capacity as passed
   or infer per-session reservations from this field.
3. **Reference preservation is not asset migration:** matching hashes establish
   consistency of supplied local files, not their origin, freshness or approval.
   Planner output does not validate storage bytes, nested file variants,
   worksheet/question relations, Notion rendering or a shared recording store.
   A future importer must re-read trusted sources and exact target revisions,
   validate authorization and record additive changes transactionally. Real
   source IDs/mappings and a separately authorized import remain outstanding.
4. **Reading preview has narrower scope:** the current renderer previews public
   Notion bodies inline. It is not a pinned snapshot of external content, a
   recording preview, all supported asset formats, or the full Phase 5 new-tab
   Student journey. External pages can change after review.
